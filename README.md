# Peter Conant

AI engineer and data scientist in the San Francisco Bay Area. M.S. in Artificial
Intelligence from San Jose State University. I use AI and automation to take repetitive work
off people's plates and make measurable improvements to workplace productivity.

## Projects

**[Agentic text-to-SQL pipeline](https://github.com/PeterConant/PIPELINE-OPTIMIZATION-OF-AGENTIC-LLMS-FOR-TEXT-TO-SQL)**
— my M.S. thesis. A multi-step LLM pipeline that turns questions into SQL. Accuracy on the
BIRD benchmark went from 35% to 48%, and latency from about 5 s to 1–2 s.
[Thesis](https://scholarworks.sjsu.edu/etd_theses/5796/)

**[Healthy Eating Chatbot](https://github.com/PeterConant/HealthyEatingChatBot)** — local
RAG chatbot: an embedding intent router, fuzzy food matching over a 2,395-food SQLite
database, and recipe retrieval, answered by Llama 3.2 1B on Ollama. Compares four prompt
strategies, including how each handled a prompt-injection attempt.

**[TinyStories Story Filler](https://github.com/PeterConant/tinystories-story-filler)** — an
encoder-decoder transformer trained from scratch to write the middle of a story from its
beginning and ending. Perplexity 11.8 on held-out stories;
[weights on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/PeterCConant/tinystories-story-filler).

**[Spotify Playlist Sorter](https://github.com/PeterConant/spotify_sort_app)** — FastAPI
service with Spotify OAuth that sorts playlists by any track field, working within the API's
100-item write limit.

**[Rotten Fruit Identifier](https://github.com/PeterConant/Food-Bank-Rotten-Fruit-Identifier)**
— a ResNet50 transfer-learning classifier that scored 99.9% on validation and 47% on an
outside dataset, and what that taught me about shortcut learning.

**[NHL Game Prediction](https://github.com/PeterConant/NHL_Game_Prediction)** — a game-outcome
dataset built from the NHL stats API, and the data leak that accounted for most of the
models' accuracy.
